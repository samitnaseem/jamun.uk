WHAT IS GULAB JAMUN?

Pronounced: guh-LAHB juh-MOON

A gulab jamun is a soft, round, golden sweet soaked in rose-cardamom syrup, traditionally eaten whole.
At JAMUN, we split them open and drip them warm with flavours you already love, like Nutella, Biscoff, and OREO.
